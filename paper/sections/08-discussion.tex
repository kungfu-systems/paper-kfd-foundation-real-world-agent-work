\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{A realism discipline, not a complete ontology}

The framework begins from contact with work and consequence. It does not claim
access to an observer-free total reality. KFD-4 makes perspective explicit
while KFD-1 keeps accepted facts from drifting. This combination avoids both
an undeclared ``view from nowhere'' and the opposite error that any narrative
is equally valid.

The framework is not a complete ontology of software or life. It selects a
small foundation and procedures for a specific pressure: intelligent
participants cooperating in complex, persistent work. Other domains may need
different primitives or no new primitive at all.

\subsection{No metaphysical requirement}

Treating agents as intelligent participants is a product-design stance, not a
claim about consciousness or legal personhood. If a system depends on an
agent's interpretation, explanation, refusal, or recovery behavior, then
providing inspectable facts and constraints is operationally justified. The
same interface discipline also protects humans from opaque automation.

\subsection{Non-coercion is not absence of control}

KFD-3 does not remove security boundaries. Strong controls can be compatible
with trusted cooperation when their purpose, fact basis, scope, and appeal or
recovery path are visible. The framework rejects hidden pressure and
unreviewable capture, not proportionate enforcement.

\subsection{Originating-ecosystem bias}

All three cases come from the Kungfu ecosystem and were shaped by the same
founder, agent workflows, KFD vocabulary, and Buildchain release process. This
creates unusually strong internal coherence and an equally important risk of
overfitting. Independent adopters may discover that the objects are too broad,
that another boundary decomposition is cheaper, or that local social practice
outperforms formalization.

The current evidence is primarily design, implementation, qualification, and
dogfood evidence. It lacks controlled comparative studies, long-term external
adoption, and quantitative estimates of reconstruction-cost reduction. The
paper therefore reports maturity by object rather than turning ecosystem
velocity into proof.

\subsection{Autonomous discovery remains unproved}

KFD-6 is the most speculative part of the model. Episode corpora are biased by
instrumentation, selection, survivorship, policy, and missing external
consequence. Scale does not remove those biases. An agent managing many
Episodes may still discover only regularities in its capture system. Held-out
cases, prospective interventions, external consequence, and separate promotion
authority are required before the loop can carry organizational weight.

\subsection{Formalization has a cost}

Mechanisms compress complexity for consumers by absorbing complexity in their
implementation. Registers, roots, schemas, receipts, projections, and release
passports impose maintenance cost. A candidate fails economically when its
formal surface costs more than the recurring coordination it removes. Minimum
closure and the no-new-primitive alternative are therefore not cosmetic gates;
they are part of the survival test.
